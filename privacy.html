privacy & cookies.

Last updated: 20 October 2026

The short version

  • We only collect what you give us in our forms, plus analytics cookies if you say yes.
  • We use it to reply to your quote request and, if you book, to carry out the clean.
  • We never sell your data, and we won't add you to marketing emails without your permission.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time by emailing info@prosharpecleaning.com.

1. Who we are

ProSharpe Cleaning ("ProSharpe", "we", "us") is a cleaning company based in South West London. We are the data controller for personal data collected through this website, which means we decide how it's used and we're responsible for looking after it.

Contact for anything privacy related: info@prosharpecleaning.com or WhatsApp +44 7821 345505.

2. What we collect

Information you give us

When you use our quote form, we collect:

  • your name, mobile number, email address and the postcode of the property;
  • your answers about the job: who you are (for example landlord or letting agent), the type of clean, property size, number of bathrooms and when you need it.

If you then message us on WhatsApp, WhatsApp (Meta) also processes that conversation under its own privacy policy.

If you book with us, we'll also hold the details needed to carry out the clean and invoice you, such as the property address and access arrangements.

Information collected automatically

Only if you accept cookies, we use Google Analytics and the Meta Pixel to understand how visitors find and use the site and to measure our ads. This can include your device and browser type, approximate location (city level), pages visited and how you arrived. If you reject cookies, none of this is collected.

Our hosting provider also keeps standard technical logs (such as IP address and time of visit) to keep the site secure and working.

4. Marketing

We will only contact you about the enquiry you made. We won't send you marketing emails or texts unless you've clearly agreed to receive them, and every message will include an easy way to opt out.

5. Who we share it with

We never sell your personal data. We share it only with trusted service providers who process it on our behalf and under contract:

  • Vercel: website hosting;
  • Our email provider: to receive and reply to your messages;
  • Google (Sheets and Gmail): to store your quote request securely and alert us so we can reply;
  • WhatsApp (Meta): if you choose to message us there;
  • Google (Analytics and Ads) and Meta (Pixel): only if you accept cookies.

We may also disclose data if required by law, for example to HMRC or in response to a valid legal request.

6. International transfers

Some of these providers store data outside the UK, including in the United States. Where this happens, we rely on appropriate safeguards recognised under UK law, such as the UK-US Data Bridge for certified organisations or the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum.

7. How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that don't go ahead: deleted within 12 months of your last contact with us.
  • Client records: kept for 6 years after our work ends, to meet tax and accounting rules.
  • Analytics data: kept for up to 14 months.

8. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • access a copy of your personal data;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • have your data deleted;
  • restrict or object to how we use it, including objecting to direct marketing at any time;
  • receive your data in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent at any time, for example by changing your cookie settings.

To use any of these rights, email info@prosharpecleaning.com. We'll respond within one month. There's normally no charge.

9. Cookies

Cookies are small files stored on your device. We only use non-essential cookies if you click "Accept" on our cookie banner. You can change your mind at any time using the cookie settings link at the bottom of every page.

10. Keeping your data safe

The site uses HTTPS encryption, access to submissions is limited to people who need it, and our accounts are protected with strong passwords and two-factor authentication.

11. Children

This site isn't intended for anyone under 18.

12. Complaints

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, please contact us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, or call 0303 123 1113.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
